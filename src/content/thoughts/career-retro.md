I started my software engineering journey in late 2019, after being dissastisfied with my current situation in life, and always having a general interest in technology.

From being addicted to [RuneScape](https://oldschool.runescape.com/) in 2007, to hosting a Minecraft server for my friends when the game was still in Alpha (2011), to infecting my family computer with viruses from sketchy Limewire songs--- navigating computers has always been close to second-nature for me. I was born in 1999, so I grew up **right around** the time where personal computers started becoming a commonplace in everyone's household. I'm still grateful to have at least a _small handful_ of memories of me playing outside on the street with the neighborhood kids---shortly before the Xbox, Playstation, Facebook, and iPod touch started taking over mine and everyone else's lives.
